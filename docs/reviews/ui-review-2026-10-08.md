# SU Card UX/UI review

This is a source-code review of the routes and components, including their dialogs, sheets, loading states, and interactions. I did not run a browser, so layout, contrast, and motion findings should be checked on devices before closing the tasks. The scanner’s valid, invalid, and success panels have uncommitted changes from the ongoing redesign; findings here focus on its surrounding shell.

## 1. Top 10 highest-impact problems

1. **Dark-mode primary actions fail text contrast:** white text on the `#52A5E8` brand token is about **2.66:1**. [button.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/ui/button.tsx>), [globals.css](</D:/SU/SU Card/app/globals.css>)
2. **The landing page promises an instant card during physical issuance:** the final CTA says the card is “ready in seconds,” and the walkthrough describes a phone-first flow without collection. [page.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/app/page.tsx>), [how-it-works.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/landing/how-it-works.tsx>)
3. **The QR Studio’s global undo shortcut can intercept typing in inputs, and overlapping autosaves can report an older draft as saved.** [editor-view.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/admin/qr-studio/editor/editor-view.tsx>)
4. **The admin mobile navigation drawer lacks focus trapping, focus restoration, Escape handling, and scroll locking.** [admin-header.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/admin/admin-header.tsx>)
5. **The shared confirmation dialog has only the generic accessible name “Dialog”; its visible title is not connected to the dialog.** [alert-dialog.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/ui/alert-dialog.tsx>), [modal.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/ui/modal.tsx>)
6. **Vendor tabs can briefly show an empty state before their first fetch.** [vendor-portal-manager.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/vendor/vendor-portal-manager.tsx>)
7. **The landing walkthrough consumes three viewport heights, including on phones, and always uses smooth programmatic scrolling.** [how-it-works.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/landing/how-it-works.tsx>)
8. **The student card truncates the holder’s name; a cashier may be unable to read the full identity from the web card.** [student-card-view.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/student/student-card-view.tsx>)
9. **Ledger summary tiles count only records loaded so far, so they can read like totals while pagination remains.** [redemptions-manager.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/admin/redemptions/redemptions-manager.tsx>)
10. **Critical actions and notices use competing modal, toast, and inline patterns; mobile toasts and several icon controls fall short of the 44px target.** [toast.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/ui/toast.tsx>), [sonner.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/ui/sonner.tsx>), [options-sidebar.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/admin/qr-studio/editor/options-sidebar.tsx>)

## 2. Findings by area

### Landing

- **High — [page.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/app/page.tsx>), [how-it-works.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/landing/how-it-works.tsx>):** The CTA and three-step copy assume immediate digital issuance. **Fix:** use flow-neutral copy, or display the current issuance mode and explain collection before activation when physical mode is active.
- **Medium — [how-it-works.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/landing/how-it-works.tsx>):** `h-[300vh]` makes a short explanation a long, sticky scroll on phones; `goTo` forces smooth scrolling even with reduced motion. **Fix:** use a compact, normally scrolling mobile layout and respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for step navigation.
- **Medium — [lanyard-card.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/landing/lanyard-card.tsx>), [lanyard-hero.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/landing/lanyard-hero.tsx>):** A 480px-tall interactive WebGL hero sits ahead of the practical explanation on small phones. The static fallback and reserved space are good, but the mobile path still carries a large visual and client bundle. **Fix:** prioritize a lighter static presentation on narrow screens and load 3D interaction on demand.
- **Medium — [current-offers.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/landing/current-offers.tsx>), [offers-carousel.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/landing/offers-carousel.tsx>):** A database failure is presented as “new offers are on the way,” which describes a different condition. **Fix:** distinguish unavailable offers from a genuine empty list; keep the public message short and provide retry when appropriate.
- **Low — [offers-carousel.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/landing/offers-carousel.tsx>):** Automatic movement stops after interaction, but the mobile carousel offers no explicit next control or position announcement beyond slide labels. **Fix:** retain swipe, add an accessible compact control or clear scroll affordance, and announce user-driven slide changes without announcing autoplay.

### Auth pages

- **High — [login/page.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/app/login/page.tsx>), [reset-password/page.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/app/reset-password/page.tsx>):** The `Suspense` fallback is `null`, so these entry pages can initially show a blank main area. **Fix:** render a stable auth shell or form skeleton at the final width.
- **Medium — [reset-password/page.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/app/reset-password/page.tsx>), [forgot-password/page.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/app/forgot-password/page.tsx>):** Buttons are nested inside links in the success and expired-link states. **Fix:** use `ButtonLink` so each action is one semantic, focusable control.
- **Medium — [choose-tiles.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/choose/choose-tiles.tsx>), [user-nav-dropdown.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/ui/user-nav-dropdown.tsx>):** Area selection and switching fall back to direct navigation after an API failure, with no explanation that the preferred area may not have been saved. **Fix:** show an inline error or nonblocking notice and make the save result clear.
- **Medium — [setup-wizard.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/app/admin-2fa/setup/setup-wizard.tsx>):** The secret-copy control is 36px square, and the setup presents both QR and secret in a dense mobile step. **Fix:** make copy and download controls at least 44px, group the manual key behind a clear fallback action, and preserve the primary verify action when the keyboard opens.
- **Low — [login/page.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/app/login/page.tsx>), [choose-tiles.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/choose/choose-tiles.tsx>):** Phrases such as “campus access,” “one tap away,” and “workspace” overstate or obscure the card’s current core use. **Fix:** use consistent language around student discounts, card activation, and choosing an area.

### Student

- **High — [student-card-view.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/student/student-card-view.tsx>):** `truncate` can hide part of the student’s name on the card, precisely where a cashier needs to verify it. **Fix:** allow two lines or scale within a defined range, keeping the ID visible and the QR quiet zone intact.
- **Medium — [student-card-view.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/student/student-card-view.tsx>), [wallet-qr-modal.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/student/wallet-qr-modal.tsx>):** The Google Wallet flow is presented on iOS as “coming soon,” while the desktop QR sheet includes a “Done” action that cannot confirm whether saving happened. **Fix:** label the desktop action “Close,” state that saving completes on the phone, and give iPhone users a direct “Show web card” path.
- **Medium — [scan-page-client.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/student/scan-page-client.tsx>), [card-scanner.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/student/card-scanner.tsx>):** `/card/scan` opens a modal camera immediately over a page whose main action is “Open Camera Scanner.” Closing it redirects to `/card`, making the underlying page effectively redundant. **Fix:** make scanning a full-page phone flow or begin with the page and open the camera after an explicit tap; keep back and close behavior consistent.
- **Medium — [deals-view.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/student/deals-view.tsx>):** “Terms & conditions” is a small `py-1` text button with no guaranteed 44px target; offer availability, terms, and schedule compete in a dense card. **Fix:** enlarge the terms target and lead with discount, vendor, eligibility, then expandable detail.
- **Medium — [history-view.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/student/history-view.tsx>):** The empty state uses a long paragraph and directs only to the card. **Fix:** shorten the explanation and offer a direct route to deals as the next useful action.
- **Low — [welcome-form.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/student/welcome-form.tsx>):** The submit button says “Create my SU Card” even when physical issuance will instead place the student in a collection flow. **Fix:** use “Continue” or mode-specific action copy; explain the next step before submission.

### Vendor manager

- **High — [vendor-portal-manager.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/vendor/vendor-portal-manager.tsx>):** Offers and cashiers start with loading set to false, so first tab activation can show an empty state before data arrives. **Fix:** initialize each tab as pending and fetch only when needed.
- **Medium — [vendor-portal-manager.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/vendor/vendor-portal-manager.tsx>):** “Export Stats CSV” opens a new window without a visible preparing, success, or failure state. **Fix:** start a tracked download and show inline status or one toast on failure.
- **Medium — [vendor-portal-manager.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/vendor/vendor-portal-manager.tsx>):** The cashier-create and password-reset dialogs rely on general error banners; the form fields do not receive field-specific server errors. **Fix:** connect errors to the relevant input, focus the first invalid field, and retain typed values after failure.
- **Medium — [vendor-portal-manager.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/vendor/vendor-portal-manager.tsx>):** “Disable” appears as an immediate row action, while revoking sessions gets a detailed confirmation. **Fix:** give disabling a concise confirmation that names the cashier and explains its effect, then acknowledge completion once.
- **Low — [vendor/layout.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/app/(vendor)/layout.tsx>):** A long vendor name shares a narrow header with the theme and account controls. **Fix:** cap the name region with `min-w-0` at the outer flex level and check 360px and tablet widths.

### Scanner

- **High — [scanner/layout.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/app/(scanner)/layout.tsx>):** The route sets `userScalable: false` and `maximumScale: 1`, blocking pinch zoom on a phone used in variable lighting. **Fix:** permit zoom and verify that the camera, tabs, and confirmation controls remain usable when zoomed.
- **Medium — [scanner-today-tab.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/scanner/scanner-today-tab.tsx>):** On a list-fetch failure, the KPI tiles fall back to zero while the list displays an error, suggesting no discounts occurred. **Fix:** show an unavailable state in the tiles or retain the last successful values with a stale indicator.
- **Low — [scanner-header.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/scanner/scanner-header.tsx>):** The offline indicator hides its text on narrow screens and relies on an icon and tooltip. **Fix:** keep “Offline” visible or provide persistent status text near the scan action.

The scan-valid, scan-invalid, and success panels should receive their final detailed review after the current redesign lands.

### Admin

- **High — [admin-header.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/admin/admin-header.tsx>):** The mobile sidebar is marked as a dialog but does not contain keyboard focus, restore it to the menu button, close on Escape, or lock background scrolling. **Fix:** build it on the shared overlay primitive or add those behaviors and reduced-motion handling.
- **High — [editor-view.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/admin/qr-studio/editor/editor-view.tsx>):** Global Ctrl/Cmd+Z and redo handlers run while a text or number field has focus. Autosave requests can overlap, so an older response can set the status to “saved” after newer edits. **Fix:** leave native editing shortcuts alone in editable elements; serialize or version saves and show a persistent save error with retry.
- **Medium — [options-sidebar.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/admin/qr-studio/editor/options-sidebar.tsx>):** Accordion headings lack `aria-expanded`/`aria-controls`, and reset/expand icon buttons use `p-1` with tiny icons. **Fix:** expose accordion state and make both icon actions at least 44px.
- **Medium — [settings-manager.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/admin/settings/settings-manager.tsx>):** Changing issuance mode is a high-impact action within a broad settings form, while a separate “switch pending students” action appears in two places. **Fix:** make current mode, quota, and affected-student counts the primary hierarchy; keep one bulk-switch entry point and explicit save confirmation.
- **Medium — [redemptions-manager.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/admin/redemptions/redemptions-manager.tsx>):** KPI tiles summarize the current loaded page and grow as “load more” runs; “confirmed discounts” and bill total can be mistaken for whole-ledger metrics. The two native selects also lack programmatic labels. **Fix:** use server totals for the active filters or label every figure “loaded records”; add `<label>` elements.
- **Medium — [students-manager.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/admin/students/students-manager.tsx>), [student-detail-manager.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/admin/students/detail/student-detail-manager.tsx>):** The student directory and detail page expose many high-impact card, status, role, and delete actions in long views and six detail dialogs. **Fix:** group actions by identity, card, and access; give each confirmation a concise consequence and a single post-action acknowledgment.
- **Medium — [batches-table.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/admin/cards/batches-table.tsx>), [generate-batch-panel.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/admin/cards/generate-batch-panel.tsx>):** Batch generation, print-state changes, restyling, voiding, and export are spread across table actions and dialogs. **Fix:** make a batch’s current print state and next permitted action prominent; show generated count and download options together after creation.
- **Medium — [vendor-offer-modal.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/admin/vendors/detail/vendor-offer-modal.tsx>), [vendor-accounts-tab.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/admin/vendors/detail/vendor-accounts-tab.tsx>):** Partner setup spans long forms, tabs, and separate account dialogs; errors are often general banners. **Fix:** use section headings, required-field cues, field-level errors, and a clear next step after creating a vendor, offer, or account.
- **Medium — [admin-dashboard-manager.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/admin/dashboard/admin-dashboard-manager.tsx>), [vendor-insights-tab.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/admin/vendors/detail/vendor-insights-tab.tsx>):** Analytics fetch after client mount and replace large regions with skeletons on first load; dashboard data age is not visible. **Fix:** preserve previous data during filter refresh, show an updating state and timestamp, and avoid resetting the whole page for a small filter change.
- **Low — [audit-timeline.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/admin/audit/audit-timeline.tsx>), [audit-detail-modal.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/admin/audit/audit-detail-modal.tsx>):** Audit entries have compact summaries but detailed inspection depends on a modal with dense metadata and raw JSON. **Fix:** put actor, action, target, and time first; collapse the JSON behind a labeled technical-detail section.
- **Low — [account-manager.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/admin/account/account-manager.tsx>), [setup-wizard.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/app/admin-2fa/setup/setup-wizard.tsx>):** Backup-code actions use toasts even when the code list or copy result is already visibly present. **Fix:** use inline confirmation near the codes and reserve toasts for outcomes that occur after the dialog closes.

### Shared UI and design system

- **High — [button.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/ui/button.tsx>), [globals.css](</D:/SU/SU Card/app/globals.css>):** Dark `--brand` is light blue, but the primary button keeps white text; the accent button also uses white on sky blue in dark mode. **Fix:** use a dark foreground on those fills or darken the fills; verify normal-text contrast in both themes.
- **High — [alert-dialog.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/ui/alert-dialog.tsx>), [modal.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/ui/modal.tsx>):** `AlertDialog` renders a visible heading inside `Modal` but passes no `title`, leaving the dialog named “Dialog.” **Fix:** connect the heading ID through `aria-labelledby`, and use alert-dialog semantics for destructive confirmations.
- **Medium — [modal.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/ui/modal.tsx>), [wallet-qr-modal.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/student/wallet-qr-modal.tsx>):** Two overlay implementations duplicate focus, scroll-lock, backdrop, animation, and safe-area behavior. The wallet close control is 36px. **Fix:** use one dialog/sheet foundation with shared focus and close rules; make all close controls 44px.
- **Medium — [toast.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/ui/toast.tsx>), [sonner.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/ui/sonner.tsx>):** Two notification systems have different visuals and stacking. The custom toast is fixed `right-6 bottom-6`, can crowd a 360px viewport, and uses small action and dismiss targets. **Fix:** standardize on one toaster, add safe-area and mobile-width constraints, and define when to use toast versus inline feedback.
- **Medium — [user-nav-dropdown.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/ui/user-nav-dropdown.tsx>):** The portaled menu has menu roles and Escape support, but no managed arrow-key focus or reliable focus return after closing. Its fixed `w-80` can leave little margin at 360px. **Fix:** use a shared accessible menu primitive or implement complete menu keyboard behavior and viewport-aware width.
- **Medium — [globals.css](</D:/SU/SU Card/app/globals.css>), [button.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/ui/button.tsx>), [student-card-view.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/student/student-card-view.tsx>):** The core palette is tokenized, yet pages repeatedly use hard-coded navy/sky values, zinc/slate surfaces, radii from 12px to 28px, and shadows from `shadow-xs` to `shadow-2xl`. **Fix:** define a small set of surface, card, overlay, radius, elevation, and text treatments and migrate them by area.
- **Low — [status-state.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/ui/status-state.tsx>), [skeleton.tsx](</D:/SU/SU Card/components/ui/skeleton.tsx>):** Many pages use these consistently, but others use raw pulsing blocks, spinners, or blank suspense fallbacks. **Fix:** standardize page, section, and button loading states with reserved dimensions and `aria-busy` where content updates.
