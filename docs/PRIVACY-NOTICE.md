# SU Card privacy notice

The Nile University Student Union (SU) uses SU Card to provide student membership cards and discounts. We collect your university email, name, university ID, and card usage records. We use these details to verify eligibility, issue your card, prevent misuse, and report aggregate program activity.

SU administrators can manage student records and investigate card activity. At a scan, a cashier sees your name and university ID to confirm your identity. Vendors see aggregate activity, not individual student records.

We keep your account while you use SU Card. Usage and audit records are retained for the SU's operational and legal needs; they are not automatically erased when an account becomes inactive. Contact SU to request access, correction, or deletion. SU will explain any records it must retain and can remove or anonymize records where appropriate.

This notice supports your rights under Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law 151/2020. For questions or requests, email su@nu.edu.eg.
